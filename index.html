Your Live Photo is ready

Two files make one Live Photo: a photo and a video that share the same ID.

Photo (.JPG) Video (.MOV)
  1. iPhone: tap Save to Photos, then choose Save 2 Items.
  2. Mac: download both files and drag them into the Photos app together.
  3. Then AirDrop or iCloud-sync it to your phone and set it as a wallpaper.
